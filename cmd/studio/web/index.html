What these do
Steps
How many denoising passes. More = more refined, slower. Each step costs the same time.
Switch at step
Wan 2.2 14B is two models. The high-noise expert does the first steps (layout and motion), the low-noise expert the rest (detail). Switching later gives more motion planning, earlier gives more detail.
CFG
How hard the prompt is followed versus the negative prompt. 1 means the negative prompt is skipped (and each step is twice as fast).
Shift
Moves the noise schedule. Higher spends more of the steps on big structure and motion, lower on fine detail.
FPS
Only playback speed: Wan is trained at 16 fps. Length in seconds = frames ÷ fps.
FLUX schnell is distilled for 4 steps; 1–2 already gives a usable draft.
